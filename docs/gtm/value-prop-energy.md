# Value prop — Energy utilities (GF-24)

## Problem

When OT/comms nodes fail, operators lose coordination paths and spend too long reconstructing what happened. After-action reviews need decision provenance, not slideware.

## Solution

Ghost Fabric rehearses **mesh handoff**, **labeled synthetic pre-event evidence**, **bounded tabletop branches**, and **human-approved recovery** with an append-only, hash-chained audit export.

## Why us (now)

- Deterministic scenario core (replayable).  
- Operator/viewer roles with mutations gated.  
- PostgreSQL-backed durable audit (restart-safe export).  
- Explicit simulation safety boundary — no autonomous targeting.

## Pilot ask

4–6 week tabletop pilot on **fictional or approved training topology**: one golden failure path, weekly rehearsal, audit pack for reliability leadership.

## What we will not claim in sales without measurement

Invented MTTR %, warning-time accuracy, or “battlefield” outcomes. Pilot KPIs are agreed up front and labeled fixture vs customer-measured.
